## 🔬 Featured Engineering & Research Projects

### ⚛️ [QuantumGuard](https://github.com/ngophatneknha/QuantumGuard) — Hybrid Quantum-Classical Fraud Detection
*Founder & Lead Researcher*
- Architected a two-tier cascade system combining **XGBoost** for high-confidence transactional classification with a **6-qubit Quantum Kernel SVM (ZZFeatureMap-based QSVC)** for ambiguous borderline cases.
- Achieved **ROC-AUC of 0.9676** on real-world financial transaction benchmarks.
- **Adversarial Robustness:** Under HopSkipJump adversarial attack, the quantum kernel demonstrated significant resilience, maintaining **65.0% F1** compared to classical RBF SVM's degradation to 8.7% (*Wilcoxon signed-rank p=0.002*).
- Presented to international mentor panels as a Grand Finalist at QC4SG Vietnam 2026.

### 🌱 [EcoCheck](https://github.com/ngophatneknha) — AI Legal Compliance for Vietnamese SMEs
*Founder & Team Lead*
- Led an interdisciplinary 5-member team developing an AI-driven platform assisting SMEs in assessing environmental regulatory compliance using fine-tuned LLMs and an expert knowledge verification system.
- Awarded **2nd Prize** at HUIT Startup VIII (2026) and advanced to the city-level finals.

### 🌧️ Satellite Spatiotemporal Rainfall & Flood Intelligence
*Lead Deep Learning Pipeline*
- **Rainfall Nowcasting:** Built an end-to-end spatiotemporal forecasting engine on multi-spectral satellite imagery using **ConvLSTM, UNet, and Spatial/Temporal Attention** (*Capstone Evaluation: 9.5/10*).
- **Automated Flood Reporting:** Engineered a data-to-text pipeline integrating **PhoBERT** and generative models producing automated hazard briefings (*RTC Faithfulness Score: 0.884*).

### 🐧 [DX-Lab Project Hub](https://github.com/ngophatneknha/OLP-MNM) — Open-Core Digital Transformation Operating System
